About

Precision transplant science from bedside to bench and back.

SNU Transplantation Innovation Lab aims to improve outcomes in kidney transplantation, xenotransplantation, and transplant surgery through integrated clinical research, translational science, artificial intelligence, biomarker discovery, and surgical innovation.

The lab brings together multicenter clinical registries, immune monitoring platforms, advanced analytics, preclinical translational models, and surgical technology to support safer living donation, longer graft survival, and more precise post-transplant care.

Research Areas

A multidisciplinary platform for transplant innovation

We combine clinical insight, mechanistic research, and computational methods to address unmet needs across the transplant continuum.

Featured Translational Model

Porcine-to-NHP kidney xenotransplantation

Our xenotransplantation program uses preclinical large-animal models to evaluate graft function, immune injury, complement activation, ischemia-reperfusion injury, and biomarker-based monitoring strategies required for responsible clinical translation.

KT

Kidney Transplantation Outcomes

Real-world outcome research to improve graft survival, patient safety, quality indicators, and center-level performance in kidney transplantation.

VOL

Living Donor Safety and Kidney Volume Analytics

AI-assisted volumetry, remnant kidney function prediction, and clinical decision support for safer living kidney donation.

IRI

Ischemia-Reperfusion Injury and Graft Repair

Mechanistic and translational studies focused on early graft injury, tissue repair, and long-term allograft protection.

XENO

Xenotransplantation and Translational Models

Preclinical pig-to-non-human primate models, immune monitoring, molecular biomarkers, and translational pathways toward clinical xenotransplantation.

AI

AI and Clinical Decision Support Systems

Predictive modeling, dynamic risk prediction, and deployable clinical decision support systems for transplant care.

BIO

Biomarkers and Immune Monitoring

Integration of dd-cfDNA, DSA, tacrolimus exposure, BKPyV, and longitudinal immune data for precision monitoring.

ROB

Robotic and Innovative Transplant Surgery

Surgical innovation in kidney transplantation, minimally invasive approaches, robotic techniques, and simulation-based education.

AI Imaging Platform

Kidney cortex AI auto-segmentation

We use AI-assisted CT segmentation to quantify total and cortical kidney volume, support individualized living donor kidney selection, and develop structural–functional prediction models for donor safety and recipient outcomes.
